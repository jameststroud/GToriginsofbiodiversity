In-class graduate synthesis presentation

Each week, one graduate student will present a short 5-8 minute Powerpoint presentation on a recently published paper on the topic of that week's classes that the student will find themselves. The 5-8 minute presentation will come after the general introduction delivered by Dr. Anderson/Dr. Stroud.

Presentations will be graded on:

  • (a) placing the recent study into the historical context of the topic [why did the scientists do the study]
  • (b) identifying the advance(s) that the recent study has made [what did they find]
  • (c) review what is still unanswered [what should they do next]

Presenters are assigned at random by the instructors.
